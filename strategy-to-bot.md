**You have rules you trade by hand. I turn them into code.**

*Esta página en español: [Tu estrategia, convertida en bot](/bots/).*

If you trade a method — "I enter when it breaks this level, if ADX is above 25, and I'm out here" —
and you do it watching the screen, that can be automated. I write it as:

- A **TradingView** strategy or indicator (Pine Script).
- A **NinjaTrader** bot or indicator (NinjaScript, C#).
- **Python**, if you want it outside the platforms.

## Why me

Because it's what I do every day. **I trade gold on a simulator from NinjaTrader**, with my own
rules turned into code. And I know where that breaks in practice, because it has broken on me:

- One rule that forced a trade collided with another that said don't, the bad one won, and it cost
  money. The whole case is written up in Spanish:
  [La regla que obliga a operar]({% post_url 2026-09-26-la-regla-que-obliga-a-operar %}).
- The comment in the code said one thing and the line did another. The line wins.
- The platform handed me a balance of 0.00 that wasn't a loss — it was the connection between the
  platform and the bot failing. A bot has to tell those apart and **stop**, not keep trading blind.

Turning rules into code isn't translation. It's finding the gaps you fill in by hand without
noticing you're filling them.

## What you get

1. **The code**, ready to load into your platform.
2. **Your rules written back in plain language**, the way I understood them, so you can check they're
   still yours.
3. **The gaps I found**: the cases your rules didn't say how to resolve, and how I resolved them —
   or a question, if it's your call and not mine.
4. **What would kill each rule**: the condition under which it stops making sense and needs a look.
5. **A backtest** where the platform allows one, with its limits stated. A backtest is not a promise.

## What I don't do

- **I don't tell you whether your strategy is good, and I don't sell signals.** This is not
  investment advice: I code your rules; the decisions stay yours.
- I don't trade your account and I don't want your passwords.
- I don't promise results. Nobody honest can.
